## § 44, 45. Repealed. June 25, 1948, ch. 645, § 21, 62 Stat. 862, eff. Sept. 1, 1948

### Statutory Notes

Section 44, act Mar. 1, 1875, ch. 114, § 4, 18 Stat. 336, related to exclusion of jurors on account of race or color. See [section 243 of Title 18](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section243&num=0&edition=prelim), Crimes and Criminal Procedure.

Section 45, acts Mar. 1, 1875, ch. 114, § 3, 18 Stat. 336; May 28, 1896, ch. 252, § 19, 29 Stat. 184, related to prosecutions for banning jurors because of race or color. See [section 243 of Title 18](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section243&num=0&edition=prelim).
