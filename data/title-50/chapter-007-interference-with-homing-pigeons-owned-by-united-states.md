## § 111 to 113. Repealed. June 25, 1948, ch. 645, § 21, 62 Stat. 862

### Statutory Notes

Section 111, act Apr. 19, 1918, ch. 58, § 1, 40 Stat. 533, related to prohibited acts affecting homing pigeons owned by United States. See [section 45 of Title 18](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section45&num=0&edition=prelim), Crimes and Criminal Procedure.

Section 112, act Apr. 19, 1918, ch. 58, § 2, 40 Stat. 533, related to possession of pigeons as evidence of violation of law. See [section 45 of Title 18](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section45&num=0&edition=prelim).

Section 113, act Apr. 19, 1918, ch. 58, § 3, 40 Stat. 533, related to punishment. See [section 45 of Title 18](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title18-section45&num=0&edition=prelim).

#### Effective Date of Repeal
Repeal of sections 111 to 113 effective Sept. 1, 1948, see section 38 of act June 25, 1948, set out as an Effective Date note preceding [section 1 of Title 28](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title28-section1&num=0&edition=prelim), Judiciary and Judicial Procedure.
