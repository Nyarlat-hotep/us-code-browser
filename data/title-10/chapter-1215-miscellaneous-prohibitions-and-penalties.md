<a id="section-12552"></a>
## § 12552. Funeral honors functions at funerals for veterans

Performance by a Reserve of funeral honors functions at the funeral of a veteran (as defined in [section 1491(h) of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section1491/h&num=0&edition=prelim)) may not be considered to be a period of drill or training, but may be performed as funeral honors duty under [section 12503 of this title](./chapter-1213-special-appointments-assignments-details-and-duties.md#section-12503).

### Statutory Notes

#### Amendments
2000—Pub. L. 106–398 inserted period at end.

1999—Pub. L. 106–65 substituted “honors functions at funerals for veterans” for “honor guard functions: prohibition of treatment as drill or training” in section catchline and amended text generally. Prior to amendment, text read as follows: “Performance by a Reserve of honor guard functions at the funeral of a veteran may not be considered to be a period of drill or training otherwise required.”
