## § 8901. Freight vessels

A freight vessel of less than 100 gross tons as measured under [section 14502 of this title](./chapter-145-regulatory-measurement.md#section-14502), or an alternate tonnage measured under [section 14302 of this title](./chapter-143-convention-measurement.md#section-14302) as prescribed by the Secretary under [section 14104 of this title](./chapter-141-general.md#section-14104) shall be operated by an individual licensed by the Secretary to operate that type of vessel in the particular geographic area, under prescribed regulations.

### Statutory Notes

| Historical and Revision Notes |  |
| --- | --- |
| Revised section | Source section (U.S. Code) |
| 8901 | 46:390b |

Section 8901 requires that a freight vessel of less than 100 gross tons be operated by a licensed individual for that type vessel and for a particular geographic area.

<a id="section-8904"></a>
## § 8904. Towing vessels
**(a)** A towing vessel that is at least 26 feet in length measured from end to end over the deck (excluding sheer), shall be operated by an individual licensed by the Secretary to operate that type of vessel in the particular geographic area, under prescribed regulations.

**(b)** A vessel that tows a disabled vessel for consideration shall be operated by an individual licensed by the Secretary to operate that type of vessel in the particular geographic area, under prescribed regulations.

**(c)** The Secretary may prescribe by regulation requirements for maximum hours of service (including recording and recordkeeping of that service) of individuals engaged on a towing vessel that is at least 26 feet in length measured from end to end over the deck (excluding the sheer).

<a id="section-8905"></a>
## § 8905. Exemptions
**(a)** [Section 8903 of this title](./chapter-089-small-vessel-manning.md#section-8903) applies to a recreational vessel operated in dealer demonstrations only if the Secretary decides that the application of section 8903 is necessary for recreational vessel safety under [section 4302(d) of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title46-section4302/d&num=0&edition=prelim).

**(b)** [Section 8904 of this title](./chapter-089-small-vessel-manning.md#section-8904) does not apply to an oil spill response vessel while engaged in oil spill response or training activities.

### Statutory Notes

| Historical and Revision Notes |  |
| --- | --- |
| Revised section | Source section (U.S. Code) |
| 8905(a) | 46:1461(f) |
| 8905(b) | 46:405(b)(3) |

Section 8905 provides for certain exemptions from the requirements of this chapter.

Subsection (a) exempts dealer demonstration recreational vessels from the licensed operator requirement in section 8903 unless the Secretary decides that safety considerations require the application of that section.

Subsection (b) exempts an offshore supply vessel of less than 200 gross tons from the licensed operator requirement under section 8904 when used in the offshore mineral and oil industry.

#### Amendments

(b) which read as follows: “[Section 8904 of this title](./chapter-089-small-vessel-manning.md#section-8904) does not apply to a vessel of less than 200 gross tons as measured under [section 14502 of this title](./chapter-145-regulatory-measurement.md#section-14502), or an alternate tonnage measured under [section 14302 of this title](./chapter-143-convention-measurement.md#section-14302) as prescribed by the Secretary under [section 14104 of this title](./chapter-141-general.md#section-14104) engaged in the offshore mineral and oil industry if the vessel has offshore mineral and oil industry sites or equipment as its ultimate destination or place of departure.”

<a id="section-8906"></a>
## § 8906. Penalty

An owner, charterer, managing operator, agent, master, or individual in charge of a vessel operated in violation of this chapter or a regulation prescribed under this chapter is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of not more than $25,000. The vessel also is liable in rem for the penalty.
