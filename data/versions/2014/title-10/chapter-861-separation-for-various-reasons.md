## § 8811 to 8813. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 3(a)(2), (b)(1), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 757, 758]

### Statutory Notes

Section 8811, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 544, provided for discharge of enlisted members of Air Force and limitations thereon, and for issuance of discharge certificates. See [section 1169 of this title](./chapter-059-separation.md#section-1169).

Section 8812, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 544, provided for discharge of members of Air Force enlisted during war or emergency. See [section 1172 of this title](./chapter-059-separation.md#section-1172).

Section 8813, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 544, provided for dependency discharges for enlisted members of Air Force.


<a id="section-8814"></a>
## § 8814. Repealed. Pub. L. 96–513, title II, § 214, Dec. 12, 1980, 94 Stat. 2885]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 545, authorized Secretary of Air Force to discharge a regular commissioned officer who has less than three years of continuous service as a commissioned officer therein, provided that such officer not be dismissed because of his marriage, unless marriage occurred within one year after date of his original appointment. See [section 630 of this title](./chapter-036-promotion-separation-and-involuntary-retirement-of-officers-on-the-active-duty-list.md#section-630).

#### Effective Date of Repeal
Repeal effective Sept. 15, 1981, see section 701 of Pub. L. 96–513, set out as an Effective Date of 1980 Amendment note under [section 101 of this title](./chapter-001-definitions.md#section-101).


<a id="section-8815-8816"></a>
## § 8815, 8816. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 3(a)(2), (b)(1), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 757, 758]

### Statutory Notes

Section 8815, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 545, provided for resignation of regular enlisted members of Air Force enlisted on a career basis and limitations thereon.

Section 8816, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 545, provided for minority discharges for regular enlisted members of Air Force. See [section 1170 of this title](./chapter-059-separation.md#section-1170).


<a id="section-8817"></a>
## § 8817. Aviation cadets: discharge

The Secretary of the Air Force may discharge an aviation cadet at any time.

<a id="section-8818"></a>
## § 8818. Repealed. Pub. L. 96–513, title II, § 236, Dec. 12, 1980, 94 Stat. 2887]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 545, authorized Secretary of Air Force, under regulations prescribed by President, to terminate appointment or enlistment of any female member of Regular Air Force, provided that appointment of a commissioned officer not be terminated by dismissal.

#### Effective Date of Repeal
Repeal effective Sept. 15, 1981, see section 701 of Pub. L. 96–513, set out as an Effective Date of 1980 Amendment note under [section 101 of this title](./chapter-001-definitions.md#section-101).
