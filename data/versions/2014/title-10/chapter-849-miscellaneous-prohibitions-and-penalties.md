## § 8631. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 7(b)(1), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 763]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 532, prohibited any officer of Air Force who was engaged in procurement or sale of quartermaster supplies from dealing in said supplies.


<a id="section-8632-8633"></a>
## § 8632, 8633. Repealed. Pub. L. 87–649, § 14c(54), (55), Sept. 7, 1962, 76 Stat. 501, 502]

### Statutory Notes

Sections, act Aug. 10, 1946, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 532, provided for forfeiture of pay during absence from duty due to disease from intemperate use of alcohol or drugs, and for forfeiture when dropped from rolls. See sections 802 and 803 of Title 37, Pay and Allowances of the Uniformed Services.

#### Effective Date of Repeal
Repeal effective Nov. 1, 1962, see section 15 of Pub. L. 87–649, set out as an Effective Date note preceding [section 101 of Title 37](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section101&num=0&edition=prelim), Pay and Allowances of the Uniformed Services.


<a id="section-8634"></a>
## § 8634. Repealed. Pub. L. 110–181, div. A, title V, § 590(b)(1), Jan. 28, 2008, 122 Stat. 138]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 532; Pub. L. 101–510, div. A, title III, § 327(c), Nov. 5, 1990, 104 Stat. 1532, generally prohibited Air Force band from being paid for performance outside air base. See [section 974 of this title](./chapter-049-miscellaneous-prohibitions-and-penalties.md#section-974).


<a id="section-8635"></a>
## § 8635. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 6(a)(7), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 762]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 532, set forth restrictions on civilian employment for enlisted members of Air Force on active duty.


<a id="section-8636"></a>
## § 8636. Repealed. Pub. L. 87–649, § 14c(56), Sept. 7, 1962, 76 Stat. 502]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 533, provided that pay and allowances do not accrue to an enlisted member of Air Force who is in confinement under sentence of dishonorable discharge, while execution of sentence to discharge is suspended. See [section 858b of this title](./chapter-047-uniform-code-of-military-justice.md#section-858b).

#### Effective Date of Repeal
Repeal effective Nov. 1, 1962, see section 15 of Pub. L. 87–649, set out as an Effective Date note preceding [section 101 of Title 37](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section101&num=0&edition=prelim), Pay and Allowances of the Uniformed Services.


<a id="section-8637"></a>
## § 8637. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 7(b)(1), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 763]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 533, provided that an enlisted member of Air Force who deserted forfeited all rights to a pension.


<a id="section-8638"></a>
## § 8638. Repealed. Pub. L. 85–861, § 36B(27), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1571]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 533, required enlisted members to make up time lost. See [section 972(a) of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section972/a&num=0&edition=prelim).


<a id="section-8639"></a>
## § 8639. Enlisted members: officers not to use as servants

No officer of the Air Force may use an enlisted member of the Air Force as a servant.
