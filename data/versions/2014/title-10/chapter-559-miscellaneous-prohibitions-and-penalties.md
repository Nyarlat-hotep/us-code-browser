## § 6112. Repealed. Pub. L. 87–649, § 14c(36), Sept. 7, 1962, 76 Stat. 501]

### Statutory Notes

Section, acts Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 381; Oct. 9, 1962, Pub. L. 87–777, § 1, 76 Stat. 777, prohibited employment of officers of the Regular Navy and Regular Marine Corps, other than a retired officer, from being employed by any person furnishing naval supplies or war materials to the United States under pain of loss of payment from the United States during that employment.

Section was also repealed by Pub. L. 89–718, § 75(6), (7), Nov. 2, 1966, 80 Stat. 1124.


<a id="section-6113"></a>
## § 6113. Loans: Supply Corps officers

Except as otherwise provided by law, an officer in the Supply Corps on active duty may not advance or lend any sum of money, public or private, or any article or commodity and may not extend credit to any officer of the naval service on active duty.

### Statutory Notes

| Historical and Revision Notes |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revised section | Source (U.S. Code) | Source (Statutes at Large) |
| 6113 | [34 U.S.C. 67](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section67&num=0&edition=prelim). | R.S. 1389. |

The words “paymaster, passed assistant paymaster, or assistant paymaster” are omitted because those titles no longer exist, and the words “officer in the Supply Corps” are substituted therefor.

The words “except as otherwise provided by law” are added because the Act of Oct. 5, 1949, ch. 600 ([34 U.S.C. 875a](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title34-section875a&num=0&edition=prelim)), authorizes advances of pay to personnel upon permanent changes of station or where such personnel are on distant duty stations where disbursements of pay and allowances cannot be regularly made, and § 303(a) of the Career Compensation Act of 1949 ([37 U.S.C. 253](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section253&num=0&edition=prelim)) authorizes advance payments of travel and transportation allowances. The words “on active duty” are supplied since the section has application to officers accountable for public funds or property. Officers not on active duty are not accountable officers.

<a id="section-6115"></a>
## § 6115. Repealed. Pub. L. 85–861, § 36B(17), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1571]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 382, prescribed a time limit for filing claims for drill pay and for the uniform gratuity. Section was also amended by Pub. L. 85–861, § 33(a)(31), which amended catchline by substituting “uniform gratuity” for “unform gratuity”.


<a id="section-6116"></a>
## § 6116. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 6(a)(2), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 761]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 382, provided that in computing length of service, no officer of the Navy or Marine Corps could be credited with service as a midshipman at the Naval Academy or as a cadet at the Military Academy, if he was appointed as a midshipman or cadet after Mar. 4, 1913. See [section 971 of this title](./chapter-049-miscellaneous-prohibitions-and-penalties.md#section-971).
