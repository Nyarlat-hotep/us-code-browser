## § 3631. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 7(b)(1), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 763]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 207, prohibited officers of the Quartermaster Corps of the Army and officers performing duties of officers of that branch from dealing in quartermaster supplies.


<a id="section-3632-3633"></a>
## § 3632, 3633. Repealed. Pub. L. 87–649, § 14c(6), (7), Sept. 7, 1962, 76 Stat. 501]

### Statutory Notes

Sections, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 207, provided for forfeiture of pay during absence from duty due to disease from intemperate use of alcohol or drugs, and for forfeiture when dropped from rolls, and are now covered by sections 802 and 803 of Title 37, Pay and Allowances of the Uniformed Services.

#### Effective Date of Repeal
Repeal effective Nov. 1, 1962, see section 15 of Pub. L. 87–649, set out as an Effective Date note preceding [section 101 of Title 37](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section101&num=0&edition=prelim), Pay and Allowances of the Uniformed Services.


<a id="section-3634"></a>
## § 3634. Repealed. Pub. L. 110–181, div. A, title V, § 590(b)(1), Jan. 28, 2008, 122 Stat. 138]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 207; Pub. L. 101–510, div. A, title III, § 327(a), Nov. 5, 1990, 104 Stat. 1531, generally prohibited Army band from being paid for performance outside Army post. See [section 974 of this title](./chapter-049-miscellaneous-prohibitions-and-penalties.md#section-974).


<a id="section-3635"></a>
## § 3635. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 6(a)(7), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 762]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 207, set forth restrictions on civilian employment for enlisted members of Army on active duty.


<a id="section-3636"></a>
## § 3636. Repealed. Pub. L. 87–649, § 14c(8), Sept. 7, 1962, 76 Stat. 501]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 208, provided that pay and allowances do not accrue to an enlisted member of Army who is in confinement under sentence of dishonorable discharge, while execution of sentence to discharge is suspended. See [section 858b of this title](./chapter-047-uniform-code-of-military-justice.md#section-858b).

#### Effective Date of Repeal
Repeal effective Nov. 1, 1962, see section 15 of Pub. L. 87–649, set out as an Effective Date note preceding [section 101 of Title 37](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title37-section101&num=0&edition=prelim), Pay and Allowances of the Uniformed Services.


<a id="section-3637"></a>
## § 3637. Repealed. Pub. L. 90–235, § 7(b)(1), Jan. 2, 1968, 81 Stat. 763]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 208, provided that an enlisted member of the Army who deserted forfeited all right to a pension.

## § 3638. Repealed. Pub. L. 85–861, § 36B(8), Sept. 2, 1958, 72 Stat. 1570]

### Statutory Notes

Section, act Aug. 10, 1956, ch. 1041, 70A Stat. 208, required enlisted members to make up time lost. See [section 972(a) of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title10-section972/a&num=0&edition=prelim).


<a id="section-3639"></a>
## § 3639. Enlisted members: officers not to use as servants

No officer of the Army may use an enlisted member of the Army as a servant.
