## § 14712. Repealed. Pub. L. 111–314, § 6, Dec. 18, 2010, 124 Stat. 3444

### Statutory Notes

Section, Pub. L. 105–303, title I, § 101, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2845, related to commercialization of Space Station. Subsec. (a) was repealed and reenacted as subsec. (a) of [section 50111 of Title 51](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50111&num=0&edition=prelim), National and Commercial Space Programs. Subsec. ## § 14731 to 14735. Repealed. Pub. L. 111–314, § 6, Dec. 18, 2010, 124 Stat. 3444

### Statutory Notes

Section 14713, Pub. L. 105–303, title I, § 105, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2852, related to acquisition of space science data. See [section 50113 of Title 51](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50113&num=0&edition=prelim), National and Commercial Space Programs.

Section 14714, Pub. L. 105–303, title I, § 106, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2853, related to administration of commercial space centers. See [section 50114 of Title 51](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50114&num=0&edition=prelim).

Section 14715, Pub. L. 105–303, title I, § 107, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2853, related to sources of Earth Science data. Subsecs. (a), (b), (d), and (e) were repealed and reenacted as subsecs. (a), (b), (c) and (d) of [section 50115 of Title 51](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50115&num=0&edition=prelim). Subsec (c), which required the Administrator to submit certain study results to Congress within six months after Oct. 28, 1998, was repealed as obsolete.


<a id="section-14751"></a>
## § 14751. Repealed. Pub. L. 111–314, § 6, Dec. 18, 2010, 124 Stat. 3444

### Statutory Notes

Section 14731, Pub. L. 105–303, title II, § 201, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2854, related to requirement to procure commercial space transportation services. See [section 50131 of Title 51](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50131&num=0&edition=prelim), National and Commercial Space Programs.

Section 14732, Pub. L. 105–303, title II, § 202, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2855, related to acquisition of commercial space transportation services. See [section 50132 of Title 51](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50132&num=0&edition=prelim).

Section 14733, Pub. L. 105–303, title II, § 204, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2856, related to potential privatization of the Space Shuttle program. Subsec. (a) was repealed and reenacted as [section 50133 of Title 51](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50133&num=0&edition=prelim). Subsec. (b), requiring feasibility study, and subsec. (c), requiring reports to congressional committees within 60 days after Oct. 28, 1998, were repealed as obsolete.

Section 14734, Pub. L. 105–303, title II, § 205, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2857; Pub. L. 106–65, div. A, title X, § 1067(21), Oct. 5, 1999, 113 Stat. 775, related to use of excess intercontinental ballistic missiles. See [section 50134 of Title 51](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title51-section50134&num=0&edition=prelim).

Section 14735, Pub. L. 105–303, title II, § 206, Oct. 28, 1998, 112 Stat. 2857, required report to certain congressional committees regarding national launch capability no later than 180 days after Oct. 28, 1998.
