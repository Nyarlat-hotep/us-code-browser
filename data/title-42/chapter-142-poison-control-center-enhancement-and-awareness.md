## § 14801 to 14805. Repealed. Pub. L. 108–194, § 4, Dec. 19, 2003, 117 Stat. 2891

### Statutory Notes

Section 14801, Pub. L. 106–174, § 2, Feb. 25, 2000, 114 Stat. 18, related to congressional findings regarding poison control centers. See provisions set out as a note under [section 300d–71 of this title](./chapter-6a-public-health-service.md#section-300d-71).

Section 14802, Pub. L. 106–174, § 3, Feb. 25, 2000, 114 Stat. 18, defined “Secretary”.

Section 14803, Pub. L. 106–174, § 4, Feb. 25, 2000, 114 Stat. 18, established a national toll-free number to be used to access regional poison control centers. See [section 300d–71 of this title](./chapter-6a-public-health-service.md#section-300d-71).

Section 14804, Pub. L. 106–174, § 5, Feb. 25, 2000, 114 Stat. 19, established a nationwide media campaign to educate the public and health care providers about poison prevention and the availability of poison control resources in local communities. See [section 300d–72 of this title](./chapter-6a-public-health-service.md#section-300d-72).

Section 14805, Pub. L. 106–174, § 6, Feb. 25, 2000, 114 Stat. 19, related to the award of grants to certified regional poison control centers. See [section 300d–73 of this title](./chapter-6a-public-health-service.md#section-300d-73).

#### Short Title
Pub. L. 106–174, § 1, Feb. 25, 2000, 114 Stat. 18, which provided that Pub. L. 106–174, enacting this chapter, could be cited as the “Poison Control Center Enhancement and Awareness Act”, was repealed by Pub. L. 108–194, § 4, Dec. 19, 2003, 117 Stat. 2891.
