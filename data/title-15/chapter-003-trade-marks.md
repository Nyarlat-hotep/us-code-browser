## § 81 to 134. Repealed. July 5, 1946, ch. 540, § 46(a), 60 Stat. 444

### Statutory Notes

Sections were repealed effective one year from July 5, 1946, insofar as inconsistent with present trade-mark provisions contained in chapter 22, § 1051 et seq. of this title. For effect of repeal on existing registrations and pending proceedings see notes under [section 1051 of this title](./chapter-022-trademarks.md#section-1051).

Sections 98 and 127 were rerepealed by act June 25, 1948, ch. 646, § 39, 62 Stat. 992, eff. Sept. 1, 1948.
