## § 801 to 827. Repealed. Apr. 26, 1937, ch. 127, § 20(a), 50 Stat. 90

### Statutory Notes

Sections, act Aug. 30, 1935, ch. 824, §§ 1–23, 49 Stat. 991, comprised the Bituminous Coal Conservation Act of 1935.


<a id="section-828-to-852"></a>
## § 828 to 852. Repealed. Pub. L. 89–554, § 8(a), Sept. 6, 1966, 80 Stat. 649, 651

### Statutory Notes

Sections, act Apr. 26, 1937, ch. 127, §§ 1–22, 50 Stat. 75–91, related to the regulation of interstate commerce of bituminous coal.

[Section 849 of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section849&num=0&edition=prelim) was amended by acts Apr. 11, 1941, ch. 64, § 1(a), 55 Stat. 134; Apr. 24, 1943, ch. 68, 57 Stat. 68; May 21, 1943, ch. 97, 57 Stat. 82.

[Section 852 of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section852&num=0&edition=prelim) was added by act Apr. 11, 1941, ch. 64, § 2, 55 Stat. 134.
