## § 91, 92. Repealed. Aug. 27, 1935, ch. 740, title I, § 1, 49 Stat. 872

### Statutory Notes

Section 91, acts Mar. 2, 1929, ch. 473, § 1, 45 Stat. 1446; Jan. 15, 1931, ch. 29, 46 Stat. 1036, set forth maximum penalties that could be imposed in a criminal prosecution for illegal manufacture, sale, transportation, importation, or exportation of intoxicating liquor, as defined in [section 4 of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title27-section4&num=0&edition=prelim).

Section 92, act Mar. 2, 1929, ch. 473, § 2, 45 Stat. 1446, provided that [section 91 of this title](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title27-section91&num=0&edition=prelim) did not operate to repeal or eliminate any minimum penalty provided by this title for first or subsequent offense.
